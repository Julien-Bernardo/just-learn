## Answers

**1. Solve 5(2 − x) = 3x − 6.**

Do the bracket first. 5 times 2 is 10, and then the minus x.

5(2 − x) = 3x − 6
10 − x = 3x − 6

Get the x's on one side and the numbers on the other. Move the 3x over and the 10 over, and flip the signs:

−x − 3x = −6 − 10
−4x = −16
x = 4

**x = 4**

---

**2. Solve 4(x + 1) = 2(x + 7).**

Brackets first.

4x + 1 = 2x + 7

Move the 2x over and the 1 over:

4x − 2x = 7 − 1
2x = 6
x = 3

**x = 3**
