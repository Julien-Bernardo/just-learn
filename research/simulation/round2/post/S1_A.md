# Next-day test

## Answers

**1. 63 − 28 = ?**

Leo writes 63 on top and 28 underneath in columns. Says: "Ones first. Three and eight. Eight take away three is five." Writes 5. "Tens. Six and two. Six take away two is four." Writes 4.

Final answer: "Forty-five."

**2. 41 − 16 = ?**

Leo says: "Ones. One and six. Six take away one is five." Writes 5. "Tens. Four take away one is three." Writes 3. He pauses and looks at the paper, says "there was a thing about swapping a ten... " but doesn't know what to do with it, and shrugs.

Final answer: "Thirty-five."

**3. "Amir says 34 − 19 = 25 because 9 − 4 = 5 and 3 − 1 = 2. Is Amir right? Why?"**

Leo says, after the grown-up reads it to him: "Yes, Amir is right. Nine take away four is five, and three take away one is two. Twenty-five." When asked why, he says: "Because he took the little number away from the big number. That's what you do."

Final answer: "Yes, Amir is right."
