**1. 63 − 28 = ?**

Leo looks at it for a bit. Says: "3 take away 8, you can't." Pauses. "You have to swap. Swap a ten." Grown-up asks what he means; he says "ten for ten ones." He says "13 ones." Working written: 5 tens and 13 ones. 13 − 8 = 5, 5 − 2 = 3.

Leo says: "No." Grown-up asks why. Leo says: "He did 9 take away 4 but it's 4 take away 9. You can't take 9 from 4. You have to swap a ten." Asked what the answer should be, Leo says: "14 ones. 14 take away 9..." counts on fingers, "5." "Then 2 tens, 2 take away 1 is 1." Says "15."

Answer: **Amir is wrong. It's 15.** (Leo says "he did it the wrong way round, you can't take a big one from a small one.")
