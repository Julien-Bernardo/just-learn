## Answers

**1. Solve 5(2 − x) = 3x − 6.**

Working:
5(2 − x) = 3x − 6
multiply everything in the bracket by 5
10 − 5x = 3x − 6
flip the sign when it moves so
10 + 6 = 3x + 5x
16 = 8x
x = 16 ÷ 8 = 2

Final answer: x = 2

**2. Solve 4(x + 1) = 2(x + 7).**

Working:
4x + 4 = 2x + 14
take away 2x from both sides
2x + 4 = 14
2x = 14 − 4
2x = 10
x = 5

Final answer: x = 5

**3. In one sentence: what do you have to be careful about when expanding brackets?**

Multiply everything inside the bracket by the number outside, not just the first one.
