**3. "Amir says 34 - 19 = 25 because 9 - 4 = 5 and 3 - 1 = 2. Is Amir right? Why?"**

He read it slowly with the grown-up's help. He said, "Yes, Amir is right. 9 take away 4 is 5, and 3 take away 1 is 2. That's what I did." The grown-up asked why. Leo said, "Because 9 is bigger than 4 so you do 9 take away 4. And 3 take away 1 is 2." He said he thought Amir was clever.

Leo's answer: **Yes, Amir is right. 25.**
