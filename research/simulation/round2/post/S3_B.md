## Answers

**1. Solve 5(2 − x) = 3x − 6.**

5(2 − x) = 10 − x
10 − x = 3x − 6
10 = 3x + x − 6
10 = 4x − 6
10 − 6 = 4x
4 = 4x
x = 1

**Answer: x = 1**

**2. Solve 4(x + 1) = 2(x + 7).**

4x + 1 = 2x + 7
4x − 2x + 1 = 7
2x + 1 = 7
2x = 7 − 1
2x = 6
x = 3

**Answer: x = 3**

**3. In one sentence: what do you have to be careful about when expanding brackets?**

You have to multiply everything inside the bracket by the number outside (both parts, not just the first one).
