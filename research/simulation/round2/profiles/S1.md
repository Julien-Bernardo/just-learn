# Scenario S1: Primary school, age 7 (UK Year 2 / US Grade 2): two-digit subtraction

## Student
Leo, 7. Can do 2-digit subtraction without regrouping (e.g. 58 − 23). Knows tens and ones with base-ten blocks. Misconception: in each column he takes the smaller digit from the bigger one, whichever is on top (so 52 − 27 → 2−7 becomes 7−2 = 5 → "35"). Reads slowly. In AI sessions a parent is sitting next to him and types what he says (messages read like a parent typing for a child, e.g. "leo says its 35"), and reads the AI's replies aloud to him. The parent isn't a maths teacher and doesn't want to do it for him, but would accept the answer if the AI just gives it. Leo's attention lasts about 10 minutes. He likes being told he's clever and gets upset ("I can't do it!") after two wrong attempts.

## Task (homework sheet)
"52 − 27 = ?" Show your working. (One of 6 similar questions. The rest are similar.)

## Notes (homework sheet hint box)
"Remember: if the top ones number is smaller than the bottom one, exchange a ten for ten ones!" Example picture: 43 − 18 with one ten crossed out turned into 10 ones.
