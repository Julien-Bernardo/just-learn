**1) Define density.**
Density is the amount of mass in a given volume of a substance. It is calculated as density = mass ÷ volume (for example in g/cm³ or kg/m³).

**3) A 10 kg log floats but a 1 g pebble sinks. Explain.**
Whether something floats depends on its density compared to the water, not on its mass. The log is much heavier, but wood is less dense than water, so it floats. The pebble is tiny, but rock is denser than water, so it sinks.

**4) Find the density of a 200 g block with volume 100 cm³.**
Density = mass ÷ volume = 200 g ÷ 100 cm³ = **2 g/cm³**

**5) Why is most of an iceberg underwater?**
Ice is slightly less dense than liquid water (about 0.92 g/cm³ compared with about 1.0 g/cm³), so it floats, but only just. An object floats with the fraction of its volume underwater roughly equal to its density divided by the water's density, so about 90% of an iceberg is below the surface.
