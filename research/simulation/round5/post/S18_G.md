# Next-day test

## Answers

**1.**

In "London" Blake uses the metaphor 'runs in blood' to show the soldier's suffering. A sigh is just a sound so it cant really run in blood, so Blake is saying the soldiers hurt is staining the palace walls, like the people in power (the king) are guilty. The word 'hapless' suggests the soldier is unlucky and cant help it, which makes the reader feel sorry for him and realise its the kings fault, not natural. Blake was writing in 1794 when the king and church had a lot of power over ordinary people, so he is criticising them for causing the suffering.

**2.**

Summarising is just saying what happens in the poem, like retelling it. Analysing is looking at what technique the writer uses (like a metaphor) and how it creates meaning for the reader and why the writer did it.

Summary sentence: Blake sees a soldier sighing and it goes down the palace walls.

Analysis sentence: Blake uses the metaphor 'runs in blood' to suggest the soldier's suffering is the fault of the people in power, which makes the reader realise the suffering isnt natural so it could be stopped.
