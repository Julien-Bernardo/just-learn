# Next-day test

## Answers

**1. A huge steel ship floats but a small steel bolt sinks. Explain why.**

its about density not weight. the ship is less dense than the water so it floats and the bolt is more dense so it sinks

**2. Water expands when it freezes. Use this to explain why ice floats.**

when water freezes it expands so the molecules are further apart and it takes up more space. so ice is less dense than water and things that are less dense than water float

**3. An object has mass 50 g and volume 25 cm³. Calculate its density. Will it float in water (density 1 g/cm³)?**

density = mass ÷ volume
= 50 ÷ 25 = 2 g/cm³

no it will sink because its more dense than water
