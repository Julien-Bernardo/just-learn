**3. 3/4 + 1/8 = ?**

8 is a multiple of 4 so use eighths. 3/4 = 6/8 (2 eighths make a quarter, so 3 quarters is 6 eighths)
6/8 + 1/8 = 7/8

**Answer: 7/8**
