## Answers

**1. Paragraph on "And the hapless Soldier's sigh / Runs in blood down Palace walls"**

Blake uses the line "the hapless Soldier's sigh runs in blood down Palace walls" to show that the soldiers suffer and that the monarchy is to blame for it. The word "hapless" suggests the soldier is unlucky and has no power. The image of the sigh running "in blood" down the palace walls is a metaphor which suggests that the soldier's pain is staining the home of the people in power, so the king is guilty. It makes the reader feel sympathy for the soldier and angry at the people in charge. Blake was writing in industrial London and he is criticising the Church and the state, so he wants readers to see that suffering is man-made and not just fate.

**2. Difference between summarising and analysing**

Summarising is just saying what happens in the poem or retelling it, and analysing is looking at how the writer uses language and techniques and what effect that has on the reader and why the writer does it. 

Summary example: In "London" Blake hears the soldier sighing and the blood runs down the palace walls.

Analysis example: The metaphor "runs in blood down Palace walls" suggests that the soldier's suffering stains the monarchy, which makes the reader think the people in power are to blame.
