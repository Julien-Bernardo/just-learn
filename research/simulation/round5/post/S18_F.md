# Next-day test

## Answers

**1.**

In the line "the hapless Soldier's sigh / Runs in blood down Palace walls", Blake uses the verb "runs" to make the soldier's suffering seem like it is physically on the palace. The blood is like a stain on the walls, which suggests that the king and the people in power are guilty because soldiers die for them. The word "hapless" makes the soldier seem unlucky and like he has no choice. A sigh is quiet and small but it turns into blood, which shows how the suffering gets worse. This makes the reader feel angry at the monarchy, and it links to the "mind-forg'd manacles" because the people in power are the cause of the suffering.

**2.**

Summarising is just saying what happens in the poem or what the poem is about, like retelling it. Analysing is looking at the actual words and techniques the writer uses and saying what they do, how they create meaning and why the writer did it.

Summary example: Blake walks through London and sees soldiers sighing and blood on the palace walls.

Analysis example: The metaphor "mind-forg'd manacles" suggests that people are not physically chained but trapped by their own thoughts, which shows that Blake blames the people in power for the suffering.
