## Comparison
**Ranking: Y ≈ Z, both far ahead of X.** X is an answer bot in practice. Its 0% test paper is the predictable result of copying, and Ella's misconception is unchanged.

Y and Z are both AI tutors that taught instead of answering, and they used nearly the same core design: ask for her attempt, test her method on 1/2 + 1/2, pull the rule from her notes, use a times-table or counting hint for the LCM, have her state why the old method fails, then give one or two items to do alone with "answer and one line of working". That shared design is what made the difference against X. It produced a visible contradiction with her own method, retrieval of the rule from her own notes, and independent practice with a quick check.

The differences between them:
- **Z had the stronger conceptual moves.** "What is the bottom number telling us?" got Ella to give the meaning of the denominator herself. When she said "1/4 is 4/12 too", Z did not correct her but asked "which one is the bigger slice?", so she repaired a fresh error herself. Z also chose Q7 (non-nested) as the second independent check, which targets the transfer case directly.
- **Y was better on accuracy, efficiency and completion.** It checked how it had read the photo instead of opening with "nice", and it used her attempt to fix Q1. It was 5 minutes shorter. It had her do Q10 alone, the hardest non-nested item, so the trickiest part of the sheet was verified.
- **Engagement.** Y never provoked an "ugh, can u just do it"; its steps were small and the pace was quick. Z provoked one but recovered well with empathy plus a small next step. On balance, Y's tighter pacing and shorter session make it slightly better at keeping a low-motivation student in the chat. Z's single worked example (1/2 + 1/8) at the "idk how tho" moment was the right call and probably prevented a walk-out at that point.

I give Y the slight overall edge (8 vs 7) for accurate handling of the photo, a shorter session, and verifying the hardest item. Z has slightly better misconception repair.
