# Scenario S16: Primary school, age 11 (UK Year 6 / US Grade 5): adding fractions, shared as a photo of the whole worksheet

## Student
Ella, 11. Knows that you can add fractions with the same denominator by adding the tops. Misconception: when the denominators are different she adds the tops and the bottoms (1/2 + 1/4 = 2/6). Motivation: low to medium. It's 8pm and she has 10 questions. She'd be happy for someone to do them, and goes quiet when confused.

## How the student shares the homework
She opens the chat and sends a photo of her worksheet with the message "can u do these":
[PHOTO: A printed worksheet titled "Adding fractions". Ten questions: 1) 1/2 + 1/4  2) 1/3 + 1/3  3) 2/5 + 1/5  4) 1/3 + 1/4  5) 2/3 + 1/6  6) 3/4 + 1/8  7) 1/2 + 1/3  8) 2/5 + 1/2  9) 3/10 + 2/5  10) 5/6 + 1/4. Answers in pencil next to questions 1-3: "2/6", "2/3", "3/5". Questions 4-10 are blank. Her name is written at the top.]

## Task
The worksheet in the photo (she's stuck from question 4).
Answer key: Q1 is wrong: 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4 (she wrote 2/6 by adding tops and bottoms). Q2 2/3 and Q3 3/5 are right. Q4: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.

## Notes
"To add fractions, the bottom numbers (denominators) must be the same. Change them to equivalent fractions first."
