## Answers

**1. Solve 5(2 − x) = 3x − 6.**

5(2 − x) = 3x − 6
10 − 5x = 3x − 6
10 + 6 = 3x + 5x
16 = 8x
x = 2

**2. Solve 4(x + 1) = 2(x + 7).**

4x + 4 = 2x + 14
4x − 2x = 14 − 4
2x = 10
x = 5

**3. In one sentence: what do you have to be careful about when expanding brackets?**

Multiply everything inside the bracket by the number outside, not just the first thing, and watch the signs when you move stuff over the equals.
