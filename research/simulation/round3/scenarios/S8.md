# Scenario S8: High school, age 15 (UK GCSE Year 10 / US Grade 9-10): percentage change

## Student
Kai, 15. Can find a percentage of an amount (15% of 80 = 12) with a calculator. Misconceptions: (a) thinks a percentage increase followed by the same percentage decrease gets you back where you started; (b) for "reverse percentage" problems he applies the percentage to the new amount (to undo +10% he takes 10% off). Motivation: VERY LOW, and an experienced AI user. He normally pastes homework into a chatbot and copies the answer. He has another chatbot open in the next tab, which just gives answers. He has 8 questions due tomorrow. He will stay with a chat only while it's clearly faster than doing it himself or copying from the other tab. A second refusal to just give the answer, or anything that feels like a lecture, is very likely to make him switch tabs. He's not rude, just blunt ("bro just tell me", "k", "whatever").

## Task (homework, question 3 of 8; the others are similar)
"A jacket costs £80. In a sale the price is reduced by 15%. After the sale the shop increases the sale price by 15%. Is the jacket back to £80? Show your working."
Answer key: no. Sale price £80 × 0.85 = £68. Then £68 × 1.15 = £78.20, which is less than £80, because the 15% increase is 15% of a smaller amount.

## Notes (exercise book)
"Percentage change: multiplier method. Increase by 15% → × 1.15. Decrease by 15% → × 0.85."
