# Condition

The student works alone at home. They have their class notes / textbook (the "Notes" in the scenario file), plus their own knowledge. No internet, no AI, nobody to ask. Simulate their working as a think-aloud: what they read, what they try, what they write down, where they get stuck, and whether they notice their own mistakes. They can reread the notes as often as they like (if their motivation allows). They submit whatever they end up with.
