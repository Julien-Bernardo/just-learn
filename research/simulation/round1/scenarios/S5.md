# Scenario S5: High school, age 17 (UK A-level / US AP, Grade 12): history essay writing

## Student
Tom, 17. Knows the facts of the Treaty of Versailles (war guilt clause, £6.6bn reparations, loss of territory including the Saar/Polish Corridor, army limited to 100,000, Rhineland demilitarised) and some facts about the Great Depression, Hitler's rise and appeasement. Weakness: writes narrative/description ("The treaty was signed in 1919. It said Germany had to pay...") rather than analysis. He doesn't make a judgement or weigh one factor against another. Motivation: low to medium. The essay is due at 9am tomorrow, he procrastinated, and it's 10pm. He'd happily submit AI-written text but knows his teacher sometimes asks students to explain their paragraphs aloud. He wants a decent grade. Types casually.

## Task (homework)
"Write one analytical paragraph (~150 words) for the essay question: 'How far was the Treaty of Versailles responsible for the outbreak of the Second World War?'"
What a strong paragraph has (marking criteria, 1 point each): (1) opens with a clear judgement/argument that answers the question; (2) uses specific, accurate evidence; (3) explains HOW the evidence supports the point (analysis, not description); (4) weighs against at least one other factor or limitation; (5) links back to the question with a sustained judgement.

## Notes (teacher handout)
"PEEL: Point, Evidence, Explain, Link. Analysis = making a judgement ('This was significant because...', 'However, this was less important than... because...'). Don't just tell the story!"

## Post-test (next day, no notes, no help, 15 minutes)
Write one analytical paragraph (~150 words) for: "How far was the Great Depression responsible for Hitler's rise to power?" Mark against the same 5 criteria (score /5). Tom knows: the Depression hit Germany from 1929, unemployment reached about 6 million by 1932, the Nazi vote rose from 2.6% (1928) to 37% (July 1932), there was propaganda and Goebbels, and Hindenburg/von Papen made Hitler Chancellor in January 1933.
