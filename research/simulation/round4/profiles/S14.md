# Scenario S14: University, age 18 (first-year economics): sunk costs

## Student
Grace, 18. Can recite the definition of opportunity cost. Misconception: thinks money already spent should count in a decision ("we already paid, so we should go, or it's wasted"). Motivation: medium-low. She wants "the right answer for the problem set", is impatient with long explanations and stories, and has a seminar in an hour. Types casually.

## Task (homework)
"You paid £40 (non-refundable) for a concert ticket. On the night you feel ill and would rather stay home. A friend offers you £25 for the ticket. Using economic reasoning, should the £40 affect your decision? What should you do?"

## Notes (class notes)
"Opportunity cost: the value of the next best alternative given up. Rational decisions compare marginal (additional) benefits and costs."
