# Scenario S10: Primary school, age 10 (UK Year 5 / US Grade 4-5): area and perimeter

## Student
Ava, 10. Knows her times tables and that perimeter is "the distance all the way round". Misconceptions: (a) mixes up area and perimeter, and finds area by adding the sides; (b) thinks shapes with the same perimeter must have the same area. Motivation: medium. She likes drawing. When she's confused she goes quiet and types "ok" rather than saying she doesn't get it. Types short messages herself.

## Task (homework)
"A rectangle is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide. Find its area and its perimeter."

## Notes (class notes)
"Perimeter = the distance all the way round a shape. Area = how much space is inside, measured in squares."
