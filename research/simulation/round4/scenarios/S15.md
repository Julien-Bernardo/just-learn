# Scenario S15: University, age 21 (first-year calculus): the chain rule

## Student
Ravi, 21. Knows the derivatives of basic functions (sin x → cos x, xⁿ → nxⁿ⁻¹, eˣ → eˣ). Misconception: differentiates the outside function and forgets to multiply by the derivative of the inside (writes d/dx sin(3x) = cos(3x), d/dx (2x+1)⁵ = 5(2x+1)⁴). Motivation: high but rushed. The exam is tomorrow, and he says "just give me the trick". Types concisely.

## Task (homework)
"Differentiate y = sin(3x²)."
Answer key: dy/dx = cos(3x²) · 6x = 6x cos(3x²).

## Notes (class notes)
"Chain rule: if y = f(g(x)), then dy/dx = f′(g(x)) · g′(x)."
