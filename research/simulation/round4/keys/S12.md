# Scenario S12: High school, age 14 (UK Year 9 / US Grade 8-9): French past tense with être

## Student
Lucas, 14. Has learnt the passé composé with avoir (j'ai mangé, il a joué). Misconceptions: (a) uses avoir with every verb ("j'ai allé"); (b) doesn't know that with être the past participle agrees with the subject. Motivation: LOW. It's a worksheet of 10 sentences, and he would normally use an online translator. Types casually and briefly.

## Task (homework)
"Translate into French: 'Yesterday, my sister went to the cinema and I stayed at home.'" (sentence 4 of 10; the others are similar)
Answer key: "Hier, ma sœur est allée au cinéma et je suis resté(e) à la maison." Aller and rester take être; allée agrees with the feminine subject (ma sœur).

## Notes (class notes)
"Passé composé = avoir or être + past participle. Most verbs use avoir. Some verbs (mostly movement) use être: aller, venir, arriver, partir, entrer, sortir, monter, descendre, naître, mourir, rester, tomber, retourner. With être, the past participle agrees with the subject (e.g. elle est partie, ils sont partis)."

## Post-test (next day, no notes, no help), with answer key
1. Translate: "We arrived late." ("we" = nous; say whether you're boys, girls or mixed if you want) (Nous sommes arrivé(e)s en retard (arrivés / arrivées both fine; must use sommes and plural agreement).)
2. Translate: "She ate a pizza." (Elle a mangé une pizza (avoir, no agreement). Tests that être isn't over-applied.)
3. Correct this sentence: "Mes parents ont parti à huit heures." (Mes parents sont partis à huit heures.)
