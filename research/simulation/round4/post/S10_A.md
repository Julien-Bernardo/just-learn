## Answers

**1.** A rectangle is 5 cm long and 3 cm wide.

Perimeter is all the way round so 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm.

Area is the two sides, 5 + 3 = 8. And it's squared so 8 cm².

Area = 8 cm². Perimeter = 16 cm.

**2.** Rectangle A is 6 cm by 2 cm. Rectangle B is 4 cm by 4 cm.

A perimeter: 6 + 2 + 6 + 2 = 16 cm
B perimeter: 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16 cm
So yes they have the same perimeter.

A area: 6 + 2 = 8 cm²
B area: 4 + 4 = 8 cm²
So yes they have the same area too. (Both are 8.)

I think they are the same for both.

**3.** Perimeter is the distance all the way round a shape. Area is how much space is inside, in squares, so you put the little 2 (cm²). Area is a different number from the perimeter.
