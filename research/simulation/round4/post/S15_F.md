## Answers

**1. Differentiate y = (4x − 1)³.**

outside is u³, inside is 4x − 1
outside derivative: 3(4x−1)²
inside derivative: 4
so dy/dx = 3(4x−1)² · 4 = **12(4x − 1)²**

**2. Differentiate y = e^(x²).**

outside is e^u, inside is x²
e^u stays e^u so e^(x²), then times derivative of inside which is 2x
dy/dx = **2x e^(x²)**

**3. A student writes: d/dx cos(5x) = −sin(5x). What's wrong, and what is the correct answer?**

they forgot to multiply by the derivative of the inside. inside is 5x, its derivative is 5. same mistake i was making (like with (2x+1)², it was off by the factor from the inside)
derivative of cos is −sin, so outside gives −sin(5x), then times 5
correct answer: **−5 sin(5x)**
