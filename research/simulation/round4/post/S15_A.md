## Answers

**1. Differentiate y = (4x − 1)³**

outside is the cube, inside is 4x − 1
outside: 3(4x − 1)²
then times derivative of inside, which is 4

dy/dx = 3(4x − 1)² · 4 = **12(4x − 1)²**

**2. Differentiate y = e^(x²)**

outside is e^something, derivative of e^u is e^u, so e^(x²)
inside is x², derivative is 2x
multiply

dy/dx = e^(x²) · 2x = **2x e^(x²)**

**3. A student writes: d/dx cos(5x) = −sin(5x). What's wrong, and what is the correct answer?**

They did the outside only (cos → −sin) and forgot to multiply by the derivative of the inside. Inside is 5x, derivative is 5. Same thing I did on the sin(3x²) one.

Correct answer: d/dx cos(5x) = −sin(5x) · 5 = **−5 sin(5x)**
