## Answers

**1. When it is summer in the UK, what season is it in Australia? Why?**

I think it's winter in Australia. The Earth is tilted so when the UK is tilted towards the Sun (so it's closer and it's summer) the bottom half is tilted away from the Sun, so it's further away and colder. So Australia would have winter when we have summer. (I think my cousin said they have Christmas in summer, which would be the opposite to this, but I'm not sure about that, it might have been a joke.)

**2. A friend says: "Summer is when the Earth is closest to the Sun." Give one piece of evidence that shows this is wrong.**

Hmm. I actually thought this was right, because it is closer in summer. The only thing I can think of is that Australia has Christmas in summer, so they would have summer in December when we have winter, so it can't be that the whole Earth is closer in summer. But I'm not really sure about that one. I think it's only the part that is tilted towards the Sun that is closer.

**3. Why does sunlight feel stronger at midday in summer than at midday in winter?**

Because in summer the UK is tilted towards the Sun so it is closer to the Sun, so the sunlight is stronger and it gets hotter. It's like standing near a fire, the closer you are the hotter it is. In winter the UK is tilted away so it is further from the Sun, so the sunlight is weaker.
