**1. A rectangle is 5 cm long and 3 cm wide. What are its area and its perimeter?**

Area = 5 x 3 = 15 cm2
Perimeter = 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm

Area = 15 cm2, Perimeter = 16 cm
