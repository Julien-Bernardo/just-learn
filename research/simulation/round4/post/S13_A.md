**1. Balance: Mg + O₂ → MgO**

Left side: Mg is 1, O is 2.
Right side: Mg is 1, O is... just 1, because there's no number after the O.
So Mg matches (1 = 1) but O doesn't (2 ≠ 1). I need another oxygen on the right. I'll put a 2 after the O so it's MgO₂. Now the right has 1 Mg and 2 O.

Re-count: left Mg 1, O 2; right Mg 1, O 2. It matches.

Final answer: Mg + O₂ → MgO₂

**2. Balance: N₂ + H₂ → NH₃**

Left side: N is 2, H is 2.
Right side: N is 1, H is 3.
Neither matches. To get 2 nitrogens on the right I'll put a 2 after the N, so it's N₂H₃. Now the right has N 2 and H 3. The hydrogens on the left are only 2, so I'll change H₂ to H₃ so it has 3.

Re-count: left N 2, H 3; right N 2, H 3. They match.

Final answer: N₂ + H₃ → N₂H₃
