## Answers

**1. Differentiate y = (4x − 1)³**

outside: 3(4x−1)², then multiply by derivative of inside, which is 4

dy/dx = 3(4x − 1)² · 4 = **12(4x − 1)²**

**2. Differentiate y = e^(x²)**

outside is e^(something) so it stays e^(x²), then times derivative of inside (x²) = 2x

dy/dx = e^(x²) · 2x = **2x e^(x²)**

**3. A student writes: d/dx cos(5x) = −sin(5x). What's wrong, and what is the correct answer?**

they forgot to multiply by the derivative of the inside (5x), which is 5

correct: d/dx cos(5x) = −sin(5x) · 5 = **−5 sin(5x)**
